Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Bleach Boys Cleaning uses cookies and similar technologies on our website.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work properly, remember your preferences, and understand how visitors use the site.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to remember that you have seen and dismissed our cookie notice, to keep the site working correctly, and to help us understand how our website is used so we can improve it.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential cookies are necessary for the website to function and to remember your cookie consent choice. Analytics cookies help us measure traffic and see how visitors interact with our site, and may be set by trusted third-party services.

Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Most browsers let you block or remove cookies, though doing so may affect how parts of the website work.
